Reporting a crash

If the app crashed, Deuce may already have captured what happened — open Settings → Diagnostics in the app. If a report is listed, attach it to your email; it makes tracking down the problem much faster.

Common questions

  • Does my data sync between my iPhone and Apple Watch? Yes, automatically — a match started on either device mirrors live to the other over Bluetooth/Wi-Fi while nearby.
  • Does my data sync between my own devices (e.g. two iPhones)? Only if you turn on iCloud Sync in Settings — it's off by default.
  • Does the app need internet access? No — everything works fully offline, except iCloud Sync (if enabled) and reading heart-rate data from Health.

See also the Privacy Policy for details on what data the app stores and where.
