Privacy Policy

Deuce — for iPhone and Apple Watch · Last updated October 14, 2026

Deuce has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server. Your matches stay on your devices — and, only if you turn it on, in your own private iCloud. The developer never receives any of your data.

Who we are

Deuce is developed by Tiago Oliveira (“the developer”). This policy explains what the app stores, where it stores it, and what you control. It applies to the Deuce app for iPhone and its companion app for Apple Watch.

What the app stores, and where

Deuce records the matches you score: scores, the point-by-point history, and the opponents, clubs and courts you add. This is stored only in places you control:

  • On your iPhone and Apple Watch, by default.
  • Your own private iCloud account, only if you turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings (it's off by default). This uses Apple's CloudKit private database, the same mechanism apps like Notes use to sync your data between your own devices. The developer cannot see or access it.

Your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange match data directly with each other (using Apple's Watch Connectivity) so a match scored on one can be followed and saved on the other. This never passes through a server operated by the developer — no such server exists.

Health data

If you allow it, Deuce uses Apple HealthKit to record a tennis workout during matches played on Apple Watch — including heart rate and active energy — and to show that data in the match report on your iPhone.

  • Health data is written to and read from your own Health app, using Apple's standard HealthKit APIs.
  • Deuce stores only a reference to the workout (its identifier) with the match. Heart-rate samples and other health data are never copied into Deuce's own storage, never stored in iCloud by Deuce, and never sent anywhere.
  • Health data is not used for advertising, marketing or any purpose other than showing you your own match stats, and is never shared with anyone.
  • Scoring works fully without Health access. You can change access at any time in the Health app or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Photos

If you choose to save a match card image, Deuce asks for permission to add it to your photo library. It cannot read your photos.

Sharing and exporting

You can share a match card image or export a match's data as a file. This only happens when you choose to, through the standard iOS share sheet, and the content goes only where you send it.

Diagnostics

Deuce uses Apple's MetricKit to keep crash and hang reports on your device, so a problem can be understood if you report one. These reports:

  • Are generated by iOS and stored on your device.
  • Are never sent anywhere automatically.
  • Can be viewed and shared from Settings → Diagnostics, only if you choose to — for example, attached to an email when reporting a bug.

Tracking, analytics and advertising

None. Deuce contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • A match: swipe left on it in History and tap Delete. If it has a Health workout, you can choose to delete that too.
  • Everything on a device: delete the app.
  • iCloud copy: turn off Sync with iCloud, then remove Deuce's data from your iCloud storage settings on any of your devices.
  • Workouts: manage or delete them in the Health app.

Your rights

Because the developer does not collect, receive or store any personal data, there is nothing held by the developer to access, correct, export or delete — all of it is on your devices and under your control as described above. If you have any question about your data, get in touch.

Children's privacy

Deuce collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.
